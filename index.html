04 / Services

Independent Owner's Agent Representation

A-104

I provide independent owner's agent representation services to projects of any size or scope for any duration as deemed necessary by the client.

Retained directly by the owner, I work only the owner's interest — not a contractor's, not a design firm's, not a program manager's already carrying a delivery incentive. The engagement is shaped by your project and your risk, whether that is one review, one phase, or standing representation from planning through commissioning and turnover.

Independent isn't adversarial. Because there is no scope waiting for me on the other side of a question, I can be straight with your contractor, your designer, and your utility — and they can take it. That is the difference between a representative who protects your interest and one who just slows the job down. Decisions get documented and issued in writing, most within two business days, because a decision you are sitting on becomes your contractor's schedule problem and then yours.

S-01

Owner's Agent Representation

Independent, owner-aligned representation at the table: scope and constructability review, contractor and consultant accountability, technical decision support, and a single accountable voice reporting to you.

S-02

Program & Construction Management

Capital program structure, phasing, and controls — budget and GMP review, contractor selection support, schedule governance, change management, and reporting an executive can act on.

S-03

Utility & Power Infrastructure

Utility and substation coordination, medium-voltage distribution, service agreements, and the long-lead power path that decides whether a campus energizes on schedule.

S-04

Commissioning & Operational Readiness

Commissioning readiness, integrated systems testing, and the operational turnover discipline that gets a facility to Day-1 ready — and keeps it there.

S-05

Schedule Recovery & Risk

Independent assessment of a distressed or drifting program: root-cause schedule review, recovery planning, mitigation, and honest reporting to ownership and lenders.

S-06

Executive Stakeholder Management

Alignment across ownership, operations, design, contractors, utilities, and authorities — with clear escalation and decision paths instead of unresolved risk.
